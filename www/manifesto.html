The Authorization Manifesto

By David Brossard · Last updated 3 November 2026

Call to arms: championing externalized, fine-grained authorization

Fellow application architects and developers,

Our digital landscape changes fast, and the need for robust, adaptive security has never been more pressing. As custodians of software integrity and user trust, we must meet the challenge of modern application development by embracing externalized, fine-grained authorization.

Why this matters

  • Enhanced security: Traditional role-based access control (RBAC) models often fall short of the nuanced needs of today's complex applications. Fine-grained authorization defines precise access controls based on attributes and contextual data, which reduces vulnerabilities and mitigates insider threats.
  • Agility and scalability: As applications grow and evolve, their security frameworks must evolve too. Externalized authorization allows centralized management, so policies can be updated and maintained without disrupting the application's core functionality.
  • Compliance and governance: Fine-grained authorization provides the granularity needed to enforce data privacy and protection regulations, so access controls align with legal mandates and audit trails are robust.
  • User experience: Users are granted access only to what they need, which reduces clutter and confusion and improves productivity.

Taking action

  • Educate and advocate: Share the benefits of externalized, fine-grained authorization with your teams and stakeholders.
  • Adopt best practices: Use attribute-based (ABAC) and policy-based (PBAC) access control, guided by industry standards.
  • Invest in tools and training: Give your teams the tools and knowledge they need to implement fine-grained authorization.
  • Collaborate and innovate: Encourage cross-functional teams to design secure, user-centric applications together.
  • Adopt OpenID AuthZEN: As a first step towards a successful externalized authorization program, standardize how your applications call your authorization service with the AuthZEN Authorization API.

Join the movement

Let us unite as a community of forward-thinking architects and developers and champion externalized, fine-grained authorization for a safer, more resilient digital future. Join the OpenID AuthZEN Working Group.

Stay secure,
David Brossard, CTO Axiomatics and Co-chair, OpenID AuthZEN
